Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

This game runs on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account and no login, and the game sends us nothing about you.

What the game stores

Two files, both on your device. One holds the run you are playing. The other holds your gold, unlocks and settings. Neither file leaves the device, and we never receive a copy.

Ads

One place only: the revive button on the death screen. Nothing plays unless you press it.

The ads come from Google AdMob. It reads the advertising ID your phone gives out, to pick what to show. Google states what it keeps at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can reset or delete that advertising ID in Android Settings, under Privacy, Ads. The game keeps working.

Permissions

The game asks for none. It does not read your contacts, location, photos or microphone.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game. Both files go with it. We hold no copy to delete.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included. It is not directed at children and its target age group is 13 and over.

If this changes

A later version may add analytics. If that happens, this page changes first and says what is sent and to whom.

Questions

Write to the developer address on the store page.
