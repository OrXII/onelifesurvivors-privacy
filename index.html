Privacy Policy

One Life Survivors · Effective 13 September 2026

Who is responsible

This game is made by Lam Duy Thuan. For any question about this policy or your data, write to unzud222@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

There is no account and no login. The game keeps two files on your phone. The progress file holds your gold, upgrades, unlocks, settings, your co-op nickname and the length of your last ten runs. The run file holds the run you are playing. We run no servers, and we never receive either file.

Backup to your Google account

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android's backup service copies the progress file to your own Google account. The run file is not copied. You control this in your phone's settings, under Google, Backup. We never receive this copy.

Ads

The game has two optional video ads, and each one plays only when you tap for it: REVIVE on the death screen and DOUBLE GOLD on the results screen.

The game contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). When the game starts, the SDK connects to Google. When an ad is requested or shown, the SDK sends Google information about your device and the ad, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, and whether the ad was watched or tapped. Google describes how it uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

The game asks Google for non-personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Privacy, Ads. The game keeps working.

Your privacy choices

In the game, Settings, Privacy options opens Google's ad privacy choices where Google requires them. Otherwise it opens this policy.

Co-op on your local Wi-Fi

When you tap HOST, the game announces your nickname and your chosen character on your local Wi-Fi network once a second, so that a nearby player can find you. The nickname is two words from fixed lists, such as BRAVE FOX. You can change it on the co-op screen, but you cannot type one, so it cannot contain your name.

During a co-op game, the two phones exchange game inputs and game state directly over the local network. None of this passes through a server.

Permissions

The game never shows a permission request. Android grants these permissions at install time:

  • Internet and network state, used by the ads SDK.
  • Advertising ID and the related Android ad services permissions, used by the ads SDK.
  • Wake lock and foreground service, declared by libraries that come with the ads SDK.
  • Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast, used to find a co-op host on your local network.

The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

The game engine

The game is built with Unity. The Unity player may send anonymous device statistics, such as device model, operating system and graphics chip, to Unity Technologies. See unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes both files from your phone. A backup copy in your Google account stays there until you delete it in your Google account's backup settings. We hold no copy to delete.

Children

The game has no account and never asks anyone's age. It is not designed for children.

If this changes

If a later version sends information anywhere new, this page changes before that version is released, and says what is sent and to whom.
